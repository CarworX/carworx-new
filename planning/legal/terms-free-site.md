### Reviewer notes (remove before publishing)

- **CPA scope.** The CPA applies to "every transaction" and to "the promotion of any goods or services" in the Republic (s5(1)(a), (b)). A "transaction" needs "consideration" (s1), which is defined widely, including "any other thing, undertaking, promise, agreement or assurance, irrespective of its apparent or intrinsic value" (s1 "consideration", para (d)). The free site may still fall in scope, and CarworX promotes providers' services. **The drafts assume the CPA applies.**
- **Intermediary.** A CPA "intermediary" acts "for remuneration or gain" (s1). In a free pilot that's doubtful, but s5(8)(b) says the Act applies whether a supplier is "for-profit … or otherwise". If CarworX is an intermediary, s27(1) requires "prescribed information" from regulations not read for this draft. **Flag for the payment terms before G-LIVEPAY.**
- **ECTA s43(2)** (review, correct and withdraw before ordering) and **s44** (cooling-off) apply to transactions; no ordering exists in Phase 2.
- **ECTA amendments.** The gazette text read is the 2002 original. gov.za notes later amendments by the Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020 and by the CPA. Chapter VII wasn't checked against a consolidated text. **Do that before publishing (part of OQ-L5).**
