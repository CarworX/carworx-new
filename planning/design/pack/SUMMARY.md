# CarworX: a one-page summary for the colleagues

**Concept, not final.** This summary goes with the presentation pack. Open `index.html` in any browser, offline. The `exports/` folder has the same pages as pictures and as one PDF. All businesses, people, prices and ratings in the pack are made up.

## What CarworX is

CarworX (carworx.app) is a South African website where drivers can:
- find car services near them
- ask questions, book a time and pay
- get straight guidance on traffic fines and vehicle admin

Businesses list themselves for free while we test the idea.

The home page has **five equal shortcuts**:

1. Wash & Detailing
2. Repairs & Maintenance
3. Glass Repair & Replacement
4. Tyres & Wheel Alignment
5. Fines & Vehicle Admin

## How booking and paying work

- **Nothing is instant.** You send a request, and the business has 2 business hours to accept it.
- When they accept, the slot is **held for you for 15 minutes** while you pay.
- You pay on a secure payment partner's page. The money goes straight to the business, and CarworX never holds it in a wallet.
- **Repairs start with a written quote.** It expires after 7 days. You approve that exact quote and then pay. Any extra work needs a new quote that you approve.
- Businesses that come to you add a **travel charge**, based on straight-line distance bands.
- During the free pilot the **CarworX fee is R0**.

## What launches first

1. **Foundations:** a private test site with made-up data.
2. **Free public site:** search, listings, enquiries, business sign-ups and fines guidance. No payments yet.
3. **Bookings, quotes and payments,** tested with test money only.
4. **Real payments,** switched on one business at a time, after legal, payment-partner and refund checks.
5. **Grow,** and test ways to earn money, each one approved by the owner.

## What the business needs to do

- **Find and check businesses.** CarworX starts with zero listings. The owner and a colleague recruit, check and support them.
- Run customer and business support.
- **Before the public launch:** appoint an Information Officer (POPIA) and agree a production budget.
- **Before real payments:**
  - set up the business details and bank account for the payment partner;
  - decide who pays the card fee;
  - decide how refunds are funded.
