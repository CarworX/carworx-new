Car care you can find, book and pay for, in one place.

This pack shows the owner's two colleagues what CarworX is, how it would look and how it would work, before anything is built. Everything here is a concept: the screens are pictures, the businesses, people, prices and ratings are made up, and nothing connects to a real service.

Part 1 · One-page summary

CarworX in plain language

CarworX (carworx.app) is a South African website where drivers find car services near them, ask questions, book a time and pay, and get straight guidance on traffic fines and vehicle admin. Businesses list themselves for free while we test the idea.

Five services, equal on the home page

Wash & Detailing
Repairs & Maintenance
Glass Repair & Replacement
Tyres & Wheel Alignment
Fines & Vehicle Admin

Behind them sit three groups: Wash & Detailing; Repairs & Maintenance (servicing, glass, tyres and alignment); and Fines & Vehicle Admin (links to the official fines channels, listed admin helpers, and towing as a referral only).

How booking and paying work

  • Nothing is instant. You send a request; the business has 2 business hours to accept it.
  • When they accept, the slot is held for you for 15 minutes while you pay.
  • You pay on a secure payment partner's page. The money is split straight to the business; CarworX never holds a wallet.
  • Repairs use a quote. The workshop sends a written quote that expires after 7 days. You approve that exact quote, then pay. Extra work needs a new quote you approve.
  • Mobile businesses (for example a wash at your home) show a travel charge based on straight-line distance bands.
  • During the free pilot the CarworX fee is R0.

What launches first

  1. Build the foundations on a private test site with made-up data.
  2. Launch the free public site: search, business listings, enquiries, business sign-ups and fines guidance. No bookings or payments yet.
  3. Add bookings, quotes and payments on the private test site, with test money only.
  4. Switch on real payments, one business at a time, only after legal, payment-partner and refund checks.
  5. Grow, and test ways to earn money only with the owner's approval.

What it costs estimates

  • Planning and the private test site: free tiers.
  • Public launch: database hosting from about US$25 a month (needs owner approval first).
  • Email: free up to 3,000 a month, then about US$20 a month.
  • Card payments: the payment partner's fee per transaction, about 2.9% + R1 (excl. VAT) when researched. Who pays it is an owner decision.
  • An attorney and an accountant before payments go live: quotes needed.
  • Marketing: the owner's budget.

Prices are rechecked before anything is bought. Nothing in this pack approves spending.

What the business needs to do

  • Find and check businesses. CarworX starts with zero listings anywhere in South Africa. The owner and a colleague recruit, check and support them.
  • Run customer and business support.
  • Appoint an Information Officer (privacy law) and agree a production budget before the public launch.
  • Set up the business details and bank account for the payment partner, and decide how refunds are funded, before real payments.
  • Look at this pack and tell the owner what works and what doesn't.
Still open: who pays the card fee; how refunds and chargebacks are funded; the production budget; who the Information Officer is; whether to approach fines partners; and, after this review, whether to start building (Phase 1). See Roadmap and decisions.

Tone of voice

Plain, honest and calm, in South African English. Say what happens next and what it costs. Never promise what we can't check.

Say: "Request sent. Gleamline has until 11:40 to reply. You haven't paid anything."

Two people, two jobs

These show how bookings and quotes would work once payments are switched on (Phase 4). The first public site launches with search, listings and enquiries only. Thandi and Pieter are fictional.

Thandi books a wash

T
Thandi
Ferndale, Randburg

Works from home on Fridays. Wants the car washed at home on Saturday morning without driving anywhere.

Pays: R330 (R280 wash + R50 travel + R0 CarworX fee)

  1. ThandiTaps Wash & Detailing on the home page.

    All five services are equal and visible straight away. Home screen

  2. ThandiFilters to "At my place".

    Bubbleworks Mobile Wash covers her suburb (approximately). No distance is shown, so their base stays private. Travel is R30–R60 by straight-line band. Search results

  3. ThandiReads the listing and requests Sat 09:00.

    She sees what CarworX checked and the prices. Bubbleworks has until 11:40 to reply; she hasn't paid anything. Listing · Waiting state

  4. BubbleworksAccepts and confirms 30 minutes of travel time.

    They see "Ferndale", not her address. Accepting puts a 15-minute hold on the slot. Dashboard

  5. ThandiPays R330 on the payment partner's page.

    The CarworX fee is R0 in the pilot. The money goes to Bubbleworks. Checkout

  6. CarworXConfirms only when the payment partner confirms.

    "Checking your payment… please don't pay again." Then: booked, ref CX-2048. Only now does the assigned team see her full address.

  7. ThandiGets her wash, then leaves a review.

    Bubbleworks' view of her address is hidden 7 days after the job.

What if…

  • Bubbleworks doesn't reply by 11:40? The request lapses, nothing is charged, and she's shown other businesses.
  • Her card fails? Nothing is taken and the slot stays held until the 15 minutes run out.
  • The 15 minutes run out? The slot is released. If her money arrives late anyway, she chooses rebook or a full refund. It's never confirmed silently.
  • She cancels, or the wash goes wrong? Refund under the business's terms, or a reported problem with a case number.

What happens when, and what's still to decide

Each phase needs the owner's go-ahead. Agreeing on the plan approves Phase 1 only, and only after this pack has been shown to the colleagues.

Key decisions already made

  • Drivers anywhere in South Africa; businesses with premises and mobile ones.
  • Five equal shortcuts across three service groups.
  • A free pilot: the CarworX fee is R0.
  • Businesses accept first; then a 15-minute hold and payment. Repairs go through a quote that is approved, then paid.
  • Fines: guidance and official links only, no ID numbers, until a partner and legal checks are in place.
  • Every business is checked by a person; labels can't be bought.
  • The Turbine logo is locked (the rest of the brand is still a concept).
  • This pack stays as local files; putting it online needs separate permission.

Ways to earn later (optional, owner approves each)

  1. A commission on paid bookings.
  2. Clearly labelled sponsorship, never on payment screens.
  3. Scheduling and reports for fleets and small businesses.

Never: selling personal data, paid trust badges, or ads in transactional emails.
