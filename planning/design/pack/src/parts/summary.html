CarworXin plain language.

CarworX (carworx.app) is a South African website where drivers find car services near them, ask questions, book a time and pay, and get straight guidance on traffic fines and vehicle admin. Businesses list themselves for free while we test the idea.

How booking and paying work

  • Nothing is instant. You send a request; the business has 2 business hours to accept it.
  • When they accept, the slot is held for you for 15 minutes while you pay.
  • You pay on a secure payment partner's page. The money is split straight to the business; CarworX never holds a wallet.
  • Repairs use a quote. The workshop sends a written quote that expires after 7 days. You approve that exact quote, then pay. Extra work needs a new quote you approve.
  • Mobile businesses (for example a wash at your home) show a travel charge based on straight-line distance bands.
  • During the free pilot the CarworX fee is R0.

What launches first

  1. Build the foundations on a private test site with made-up data.
  2. Launch the free public site: search, business listings, enquiries, business sign-ups and fines guidance. No bookings or payments yet.
  3. Add bookings, quotes and payments on the private test site, with test money only.
  4. Switch on real payments, one business at a time, only after legal, payment-partner and refund checks.
  5. Grow, and test ways to earn money only with the owner's approval.

What it costs estimates

  • Planning and the private test site: free tiers.
  • Public launch: database hosting from about US$25 a month (needs owner approval first).
  • Email: free up to 3,000 a month, then about US$20 a month.
  • Card payments: the payment partner's fee per transaction, about 2.9% + R1 (excl. VAT) when researched. Who pays it is an owner decision.
  • An attorney and an accountant before payments go live: quotes needed.
  • Marketing: the owner's budget.

Prices are rechecked before anything is bought. Nothing in this pack approves spending.

What the business needs to do

  • Find and check businesses. CarworX starts with zero listings anywhere in South Africa. The owner and a colleague recruit, check and support them.
  • Run customer and business support.
  • Appoint an Information Officer (privacy law) and agree a production budget before the public launch.
  • Set up the business details and bank account for the payment partner, and decide how refunds are funded, before real payments.
  • Look at this pack and tell the owner what works and what doesn't.
