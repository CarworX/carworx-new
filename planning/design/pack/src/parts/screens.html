Each group shows the main screen on a 360-pixel-wide phone, then the other states it must handle: waiting, expiring, failing, empty and paused. All businesses, people, prices and ratings are fictional.

05Checkout and what happens after

Once the business accepts, the slot is held for 15 minutes. Payment happens on the payment partner's secure page. We confirm only when the payment partner tells us the money arrived, never just because the browser came back. In this pack nothing is charged: payments would run in test mode.
