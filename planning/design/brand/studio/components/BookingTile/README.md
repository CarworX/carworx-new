# BookingTile

The confirmation object: a blue tick, what happened, who, when, and a dashed meta row.

- **Accepted** (the business said yes; a 15-minute hold starts) and **Booked and paid** (the payment partner confirmed).
- Only show "Booked and paid" after the payment partner confirms, never because the browser came back.
- The meta row ends with who was paid: "Paid to the business". CarworX never holds the money.
