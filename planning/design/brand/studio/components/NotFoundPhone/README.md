# NotFoundPhone

The NotFound page at 390 × 844: the same page and code, laid out for a phone.

- The numerals and loupe scale down (loupe radius 74 px); the copy, pill and link stay full size; the five chips lose their objects and wrap into three rows.
- The whole page fits 390 × 844 without scrolling, so touch tracking can take the screen (`touch-action: none` only when it fits).
- Touch: the loupe follows your finger; a tap sends the pulse.
