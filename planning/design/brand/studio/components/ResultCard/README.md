# ResultCard

One business in search results: thumbnail, name, how far (straight-line, and it says so), what CarworX checked, and the starting price.

- A business that comes to you shows **no distance** ("your suburb is in their area, approx."): it would reveal its base.
- Labels say exactly what was checked and how; they can't be bought.
- The thumbnail is the business's own photo; until there is one, a hand-drawn line thumbnail on a tint.
- Fictional businesses carry DEMO; ratings in demos say "demo".
