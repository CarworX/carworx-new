# SiteFooter

One footer for every public page, including sign in and the fines pages: the big lockup, one link row and a fine-print row.

## Anatomy
1. Horizontal ink lockup, up to 520px wide (80% on phones).
2. Link row: Find a service · Fines · For businesses · List your business · Contact support.
3. Fine row with a hairline above: "carworx.app · South Africa" on the left; Contact support · Privacy · Terms of use (underlined) on the right. On phones both halves centre and stack.

## States
One state. It never changes with sign-in.

## Tokens used
`--space-16`, `--space-12`, `--space-10`, `--space-8`, `--space-6`, `--hair`, `--graphite`, `--muted`, `--blue-ink` (link hover).

## Do / Don't
- Do use it on every public page; replaces the landing, `SitesShell` and `PublicShell` footers.
- Don't add social icons, newsletters or a second logo.
- Don't put legal words in the link row; they live in the fine row.
