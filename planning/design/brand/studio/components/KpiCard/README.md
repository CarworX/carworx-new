# KpiCard

The business dashboard's headline numbers: large thin figures (`kpi`, Geist 200) on frosted cards over a static blue-to-orange glow.

- Four at most in a row: requests, paid directly, average reply against the 2-hour promise, and the CarworX fee.
- Money shows the currency prefix small (`<sub>R</sub>3 960`); times as 1h07.
- On phones the cards stack as rows: label left, number right.
- The glow is static: never animate it or put moving content under a blur.
- Numbers in concepts are demo data; label them so.
