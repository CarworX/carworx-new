# 13. Register as Information Officer with the Information Regulator

**Why:** you are CarworX's Information Officer (D-043). POPIA says officers "must take up their duties … only after the responsible party has registered them with the Regulator" (POPIA s55(2)). G-PROD requires it (PLAN §18). The Regulator's portal says: "Registration is not optional."

**When:** before G-PROD. It doesn't depend on any code. Registering early is better, because the breach-report portal only works for registered officers (incident plan, step 4).

**Time:** about 20 minutes. "Registration is processed immediately. No manual approval step is required."

**Cost:** free.

**Agents never register for you** (D-043). Your ID number goes into the Regulator's form only.

Sources, read 2026-11-10: the Regulator's [eServices Information Officers page](https://eservices.inforegulator.org.za/officers/default.aspx) and the [Guidance Note on Information Officers](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/InfoRegSA-GuidanceNote-IO-DIO-20210401.pdf) (1 April 2021).

**Legal-source update, 2026-11-11:** read the 2021 Guidance Note together with [GN 6126, GG 52523](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/notices/2025/20250417-gg52523gon6126-POPIA-Regulations-Amendment.pdf) and its [linked final Regulations](https://inforegulator.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/POPIA-2021-Regulations-FINAL-21-Jan-2025.pdf). The notice gives the amendments immediate effect from 17 April 2025. Some duties listed in the older guidance have changed; registration under POPIA s55(2) remains required.

## Before you start: one decision

**Which organisation do you register? (OQ-L1)** The company isn't registered yet (D-049). Under POPIA a "private body" includes "a natural person who carries or has carried on any trade, business or profession" (POPIA s1). For that kind of body, the Information Officer is the natural person, or someone they authorise (Guidance Note, para 5.1). The portal form asks for the organisation's "registered name, registration number, type, and address".

- **If the company is registered by then:** register the company, with yourself as Information Officer.
- **If not:** pick the portal's organisation type that fits a business run by individuals, and use CarworX as the name. **If no type fits, stop and tell Claude** what types the form offers. Don't guess. You can update or re-register when the company exists. The portal has **Name Changes** and **Organisation Deregistration** for that.

## Steps

1. Go to <https://eservices.inforegulator.org.za> and select **Register** to create your portal login. Use your own email address and a strong, unique password with your password manager.
2. Log in. Open **Services → Information Officers → New Registration**.
3. **Organisation details:** name, registration number (if any), type and address. Use the same address you'll publish in the privacy notice (OQ-L2).
4. **Information Officer details:** your full name, ID number, designation (for example "Founder"), appointment date (today) and contact details.
5. **Deputy and Admin Officers (recommended):** add one cofounder as **Admin Officer**. The portal warns that "an organisation with only one registered user risks being locked out entirely". Adding them as **Deputy Information Officer** is optional for a private body. Note the Guidance Note says "only employee(s) of a body can be designated as a Deputy Information Officer" (para 7.2).
6. **Review and submit.** The certificate is emailed to you. You can download it again under **My Organisations → Download Certificate**.

## After registering

- **Keep it current.** Update the details on the portal when anything changes, and check them at least once a year. The Guidance Note says to update "at intervals of not more than one year" (para 11.1).
- **Your other duties** under regulation 4 include a compliance framework that is continually improved, a personal information impact assessment, internal measures and systems for requests, and awareness sessions. These are operational duties: drafting the legal documents alone does not complete them. The 2025 amendment adds continual improvement to regulation 4(1)(a) and deletes regulation 4(1)(c) and 4(2), so the 2021 Guidance Note's PAIA-manual duties (paras 6.2.3 and 6.2.6) are no longer current duties under that regulation. **This does not decide whether PAIA s51 independently requires a manual.** Keep OQ-L7 open until that separate assessment is complete.

## Check it worked

- **My Organisations** lists CarworX with you as Information Officer.
- The certificate shows the organisation name and the date.
